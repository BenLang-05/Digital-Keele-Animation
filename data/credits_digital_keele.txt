Digital Keele

Sprite animation by Benjamin Lang: featuring:

SCENE ONE:
Digital Keele animation

A squirrel runs through the Keele university Student Union square,
He runs towards a swaying cherry blossom tree where another squirrel sits,
While the moving clouds rain down the binary rain on him.

Graphical elements/media: 
Both squirrel images and backgrounds loaded with loadImage() and drawn with image()
Colour given to the binary rain with fill() and drawn with text()
The cherry blossom tree drawn with noStroke() and rect() and coloured with fill()
And positioned with translate()
Branches are drawn and animated with rotate(), noise(), rect() and translate()
While the blossom is drawn with noStroke(), fill() and ellipse()

Programming:
For loops to split up the squirrel sprites, 
moving the backgrounds,
randomising the position for the rain to fall from.
Selection to create the branches of the trees.

SCENE TWO:
Credits
Vertically scrolling centred credits displayed with text() and colour blue using fill(). 
Credits text extracted from notepad using text() and textAlign().
